## ✨ Features & Highlights

- **Kawaii 3D Table Tennis Court**: Clean, vibrant 3D arena with wooden table top, court lines, and low-poly 3D environment.
- **Kawaii Eye Paddles**: Adorable animated paddles with expressive blinking kawaii eyes that react to every rally!
- **Generous & Forgiving Hitboxes**: Balanced paddle collision bounding boxes that make hitting the ball intuitive, satisfying, and responsive on both mouse and touch.
- **1-to-1 Mouse Paddle Tracking**: The paddle glides directly with your mouse cursor across the table, eliminating input lag.
- **Curve-Ball Spin Physics**: Apply slice and spin by moving the paddle while striking the ball, altering its trajectory across the net.
- **Adaptive AI Opponent**: Smart AI that adjusts to your rally tempo, with natural reaction speed and error margins.
- **Retro Web Audio SFX**: Procedural ping-pong bounces, net clips, edge hits, and scoring fanfares.
- **Arcade Leaderboard Sync**: Automatically persists and submits your rally streaks and match victories to the Marcus Arcade Hub.

## 🕹️ Controls

| Action | Desktop / Mouse | Mobile / Touchscreen |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Move Paddle** | Move Mouse Cursor | Drag Finger across player side |
| **Serve Ball** | Click Mouse Button / Space | Tap Screen |
| **Apply Spin** | Slice paddle horizontally during impact | Swipe paddle across ball during impact |
| **Pause Game** | Press `[ESC]` or `[P]` | Tap Pause Button |
